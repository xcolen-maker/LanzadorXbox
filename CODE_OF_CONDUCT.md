# Code of Conduct

We want Lanzador Xbox to be a friendly place for everyone who uses it or helps with it,
whatever their experience, country, language, gender, age or background.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or whose first language is not
  yours.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that helps: say what is wrong and, if you can, how to fix it.
- Assume good intentions, and say so when you are not sure what someone meant.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Posting other people's personal information.
- Spam and unrelated advertising.
- Asking for, or sharing, pirated games or other copyrighted files.

## What happens when it is broken

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these
rules, and may block people who keep doing it.

## How to report a problem

Open an [issue](../../issues) that describes what happened, or use GitHub's **Report
content** option on the comment. Reports are handled with care and kept private whenever
GitHub allows it.

This applies to the issues, pull requests and discussions of this repository.
